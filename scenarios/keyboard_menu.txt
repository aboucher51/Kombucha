# The pause menu is reachable without a mouse: open() grabs focus, ui_*
# actions walk to Resume, and the focus ring makes the walk visible (an
# HSlider has no focus stylebox in Godot, so the ring shows on the button).
# A pad drives exactly these actions (Pads synthesises them), so this is
# the pad path too.
wait 10
press pause
assert_visible PausedLabel
press ui_down
press ui_down
press ui_down
shot focus-ring 440 220 400 280
press ui_accept
expect_fail assert_visible ResumeButton
